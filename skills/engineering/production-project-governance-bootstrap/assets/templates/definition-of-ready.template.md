# Definition of Ready

- Status: Active
- Owner: `[delivery governance owner]`

A non-trivial item is ready when applicable checks are evidenced:

- [ ] Problem, user/system outcome, and source of truth are clear.
- [ ] Acceptance criteria are testable and non-goals are explicit.
- [ ] Public, data, security, reliability, and observability impacts are classified.
- [ ] Architecture questions are resolved or explicitly deferred with owner and consequence.
- [ ] Dependencies, risk level, approvals, migration, rollout, and rollback implications are known.
- [ ] Verification commands or manual checks are confirmed.

When not ready, allow investigation, clarification, specification, design proposals, and decision discovery. Do not guess critical business rules and proceed as approved.
